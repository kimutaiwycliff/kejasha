---
title: Security
description: How tenants, money and secrets are protected, and how to report a problem.
---

- **Isolation:** every organization's rows are protected by Postgres row-level security; the
  application connects with a role that cannot bypass it. A test suite proves cross-tenant access
  fails for every table.
- **Money:** Kejasha never holds funds. M-Pesa is booked once per transaction and only after
  Safaricom confirms STK payments; Paystack webhooks are signature-checked and re-verified.
- **Secrets:** Daraja credentials and saved card authorizations are envelope-encrypted
  (AES-256-GCM) and never returned by any API. Logs and error reports never contain them.
- **Access:** roles from owner to read-only viewer; caretakers are limited to their properties;
  operators are logged on every request and sit behind SSO.
- **Abuse:** sign-in, codes, payments prompts, webhooks, uploads and exports are rate-limited;
  uploads are size-limited; iCal fetching cannot reach private networks.

The latest review and how each finding was handled: `docs/security/review-2026-10.md` in the
repository.
