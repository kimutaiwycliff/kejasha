Short-stay units (`mode: "short_stay"`) take bookings alongside long-term units in one portfolio.

## Rates and quotes

`PUT /v1/units/{unitId}/rate-plan` sets the base and weekend nightly rates, minimum stay, buffer
days and cleaning fee. `POST /v1/units/{unitId}/seasons` adds seasonal rates; seasons on a unit
can't overlap. `GET /v1/units/{unitId}/quote?checkIn=…&checkOut=…` prices a stay night by night
and says whether it's free.

## Bookings — double-booking is impossible

`POST /v1/bookings` books a unit from any source (`direct`, `walk_in`, `whatsapp`, `airbnb`,
`booking`, `other`). The database itself refuses overlapping stays and blocks: a clash answers
`409 dates_unavailable`, even when two requests race. `checkOut` is the departure day; buffer
days after it stay blocked for cleaning.

Directly collected bookings (direct, walk-in, WhatsApp, other) create an amount owed, paid by
Paybill, STK or cash like rent. Airbnb and Booking.com bookings are paid out by the platform; you
record their gross and commission for profit reporting.

## Airbnb / Booking.com calendars (read-only)

Connect a unit's iCal export URL with `POST /v1/ical-feeds`. Kejasha imports reservations as
bookings and "not available" dates as blocks, every 20 minutes or on demand
(`POST /v1/ical-feeds/{id}/sync`). If an imported stay clashes with one you already have, it's
listed under the feed's `conflicts` — nothing is overwritten. Feed URLs are secrets: only a
masked hint is ever returned.

## Profit

`GET /v1/reports/short-stay?period=YYYY-MM` gives per unit: nights sold, occupancy, average
daily rate, RevPAR, gross, commission, expenses and net — plus a channel comparison showing what
each platform really costs.
