---
title: Tenant portal
description: Tenants see their rent, receipts and repairs, and pay by M-Pesa.
---

A tenant signs in with their phone and an SMS code. Kejasha finds their tenancies with **every**
landlord on the platform that holds that verified phone number.

| Endpoint | What |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/portal/me` | Tenancies and leases with balances, per landlord |
| `GET /v1/portal/leases/{leaseId}/statement` | Invoices and payments with a running balance |
| `POST /v1/portal/leases/{leaseId}/pay` | An M-Pesa prompt to the tenant's phone (the landlord's own Paybill/Till) |
| `GET /v1/portal/payments/{id}/receipt` | Receipt PDF |
| `GET /v1/portal/tickets`, `POST /v1/portal/tickets` | Repairs the tenant reported |

A lease is visible only to the person whose phone is on its tenant record; anything else answers
`404`.
